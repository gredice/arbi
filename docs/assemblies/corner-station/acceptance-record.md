station and link approved calculations, as-built drawings, print records and

Stop before loaded installation while printed-process qualification, compatible
pin, fitted keeper, guy attachment, structural/site review, soil/anchor tests or
stage authorization is missing. An unloaded bench fit mock-up may record
measurements under the reviewed procedure; it does not clear loaded/overhead
acceptance. Preserve failed results and discarded parts as evidence.

For a historical metal variant, record the actual angle/bend/factory holes,
metal-saddle grade/process/contact, plate perforations, corrosion and its own
fastener stack as additional variant-specific evidence. Metal geometry or its
earlier nominal checks do not qualify the printed head.
