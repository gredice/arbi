# Corner support set completion proposal

8 October 2026. Revision `0.1.0`, `concept-unvalidated`.

The owner requested completion of the Corner support set. This proposal keeps
the through-bolted steel-angle load path and adds its missing dimensioned head,
round-pole seating concept, removable weather shields, marking template,
assembly instructions, calculation inputs and acceptance record. It becomes
the repository's nominal design package only on merge. It does not approve an
installed structure or replace the [split-collar alternative](../../../hardware/assemblies/corner-station/pole-pulley-mount.md).

The square 100 mm arrangement retains the historical 150 mm angle, stacked
backing plates and M12 bolts as a comparison. Round 100/120/140 mm proposed
arrangements replace the 150 mm angle with a 200 mm angle and add two machined
metal saddles; they require matching longer bolts and reviewed manufacture.
The longer angle places the sheave's downward tangent over the committed winch
tangent; the original round-120 arrangement has a 42.55 mm depth mismatch.
There is no printed material in the primary load path. Removable white shields
follow ADR-0007, attach with two stem/rear straps and an independent roof strap, and stop short
of the pulley so its line and connection stay inspectable. They are weather
shields, not a line keeper or an IP-rated enclosure.

The received pulley is recorded as Barton BA01090, a 30 mm plain-bearing
double-tang block. Supplier envelopes do not establish the received pin, tang
gap, articulation or thin-line retention. A generic connector is explicitly a
layout allowance. Do not enlarge the block's factory holes or force an M8
shackle into it. The earlier 58 mm keeper does not fit this package and must
not be counted as a completed guard.

The whole station also needs a surveyed post/anchor layout, reviewed guy-to-post
connection, timber/soil/anchor capacities, maximum line tensions, winch alignment,
the powered corner's electrical route and the dock corner's combined loads.
These inputs remain open; the package includes the checks and measurement record
needed to close them without assigning unsupported loads or installation limits.
