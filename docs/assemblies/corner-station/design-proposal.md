# Corner support set printed-head proposal

8 October 2026. New printed models are revision `0.1.0`, `concept-unvalidated`.

The owner selected a mostly 3D-printed corner head with ordinary bought metal
fasteners and the received marine pulley. The proposed head uses two printed,
ribbed carrier halves with integrated curved post adapters, two separate rear
post pads, removable front/rear covers and one shared marking tool. It requires
no custom metal angle, machined saddles or fabricated backing plates. This is a
new load-bearing printed design, not a plastic reproduction of the steel angle.
It becomes the selected repository design only on merge; it assigns no installed
load capacity and does not authorize loaded operation.

Two M12 through-bolts connect the carrier and rear pads through the timber.
Two bought M8 cross-bolts connect the carrier halves. A compatible bought pin
connects the received pulley to the printed terminal lug. The carrier's triangular
side webs and top chord carry pulley load; the removable covers and their two
straps remain non-structural. The default is round-120 with round-100/140 and
square-100 studies. The passive 1.5 mm and provisional powered 4.5 mm line
positions match the existing winch tangents in a provisional freely articulating
45° block pose for equal leg tensions and a horizontal span. Received
articulation, unequal tensions and other span angles remain unverified.

The historical square-post 150 mm steel angle and the proposed round-post
200 mm angle with machined metal saddles remain separate alternatives with
their existing sources and revisions. Their drilling, bolt lengths and covers
must not be mixed into the printed kit. The
[split-collar alternative](../../../hardware/assemblies/corner-station/pole-pulley-mount.md)
also remains separate. The printed head retains positive through-bolt attachment
and does not depend on an unqualified friction-only collar.

The received pulley record identifies Barton BA01090, a 30 mm plain-bearing
double-tang block. The new 8 mm printed lug, provisional 9 mm tang gap and pin
envelope need received measurements. Do not force a pin, widen the tangs or
drill the block. The earlier 58 mm keeper remains unfitted; neither printed
cover is a completed thin-line keeper.

Material, print orientation, slicing, conditioning, fastener-seat pressure,
anisotropic strength, sustained-load creep, weathering and replacement criteria
must be qualified together. PAHT-CF or tested ASA are candidates, not approved
structural materials. The [design package](design-package.md) records prototype
process starting points and the [load-study note](printed-load-study.md) describes
load and material assumptions. The [acceptance record](acceptance-record.md)
records the measurements and tests needed before installation. Maximum line
tension, dynamic/fault loads, received-pulley fit, guy attachment, timber, soil,
anchor, powered-line routing and dock loads remain open.
