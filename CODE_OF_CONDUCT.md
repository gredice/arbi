# Code of Conduct

ARBI is a shared open engineering workspace. We expect contributors and maintainers to keep collaboration professional, respectful, evidence-based, and focused on useful outcomes.

## Our standards

Behavior that supports the project includes:

- treating contributors, maintainers, users, and community members with respect;
- giving and receiving technical feedback with clarity and patience;
- distinguishing disagreement about evidence from judgment of a person;
- respecting different levels of experience, language fluency, and domain familiarity;
- crediting work accurately and protecting private information;
- raising physical safety, security, and reliability concerns without retaliation.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, intimidation, stalking, threats, or sustained disruption;
- insults, personal attacks, discriminatory or demeaning comments;
- sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- deliberately derailing project discussions or retaliating against a report;
- pressuring others to approve, build, or operate a design despite unresolved safety concerns.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, project communication, meetings, events, and other spaces where someone represents ARBI or Gredice.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through a private channel they have made available. If no private channel is available, open a minimal public issue asking a maintainer to arrange private contact. Do not include sensitive incident details in that issue.

For software or hardware security vulnerabilities, use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Maintainers will limit sensitive information to people who need it to understand and respond. They may remove content, limit participation, reverse project actions, or take other proportionate steps needed to protect the community and project.
