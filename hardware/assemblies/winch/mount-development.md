# Winch mount development proposal — 2026-09-08

Status: proposed concept implementation, not an accepted field installation.
Owner request: continue from the segmented drum to its bearing/motor mount;
PLA prototype first, ASA later. Direct drive, two external 608 bearings, the
selected 8 mm rod and ordered coupling remain the interfaces.

Use a common rigid metal backplate, two removable split bearing supports, an
axially adjustable motor bracket and a removable coupling cover. A long printed
beam is not assumed to hold the bearing axes straight. The same printed parts
serve passive and powered drums; support spacing follows the drum width.

The base is a drilling/cutting reference for unquoted metal stock. Four centered
through-bolts and a backing plate propose attachment to the flat face of a nominal
100 mm timber post. Actual timber, plate bending, bolt loads, retention and round
post adapters need installation review. This proposal does not alter the top
pulley bracket or qualify either long shaft. Full-travel line guidance, outdoor
cover, homing and powered slip-ring integration remain separate unfinished work.

Small metal inner-ring spacers are added between collars and bearings to avoid
seal/outer-ring contact. This changes contextual shaft layout, not printed drum
interfaces. See [mount geometry and assembly](mount.md) for dimensions and evidence.
