# ARBI passive winch desk feet - revision 0.1.0

## Print list

| File | Quantity | Size (mm) | Installation |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| winch-desk-foot-short-r0.1.0.stl | 2 | 160 x 60 x 35 | Non-motor pair of post holes; toes point away from motor |
| winch-desk-foot-long-r0.1.0.stl | 2 | 244 x 60 x 35 | Motor-side pair of post holes; toes point toward motor |

STL units are millimetres. Import at 100% scale. Each STL contains one foot,
already flat on its sole at Z=0. Print two copies of each. Do not mirror them.
The long part fits a 256 x 256 mm bed, including a brim up to 5 mm wide if the
slicer's usable area permits. Two copies can be placed side by side.

## Fit and clearance

- Intended base: passive winch, 550 x 180 x 8 mm aluminium.
- Attach through the four existing 9 mm post holes, a 50 x 120 mm rectangle.
- Hole centres from the plate's non-motor end and a long edge:
  (148.45,30), (148.45,150), (198.45,30), (198.45,150) mm.
- Plate underside sits 35 mm above the desk. The ribbed soles are 5 mm thick.
- Existing bearing/motor hardware was checked with a 20 mm diameter envelope
  extending 25 mm below the plate: no intersection with the feet.
  Measure your longest protrusion; keep it at or below 25 mm.
- Each foot has a 9.2 mm M8 clearance hole and a 26 mm diameter underside
  access recess, 27 mm deep. The load-bearing roof is 8 mm thick.
- These extended feet spread support along the plate. They do not provide a
  separate contact point under the far end of the motor.
- The support geometry is specific to the passive base. The longer powered
  base has not been checked with these feet.

## Hardware

- 4 x M8 x 35 mm bolts (length measured below the head).
- 8 x standard M8 flat washers, approximately 16 mm outside diameter.
- 4 x M8 nuts, preferably nyloc.

The supplied 35 mm bolt length assumes an 8 mm plate. For this stack the tip
remains about 10 mm above the desk. Long pole-mounting bolts are unsuitable.
Use a socket to hold the underside nut while tightening the bolt from above.
Washers distribute pressure onto the plastic; tighten only enough to hold the
feet securely without deforming them.

## Slicer starting settings

- PLA or PETG for an indoor bench prototype.
- 0.20 mm layers, 5 walls, 40% infill, 6 top/bottom layers.
- Keep the broad sole on the bed as exported.
- Enable support from the build plate inside the round underside nut recess.
  Its flat ceiling is 27 mm up; remove this support before fitting the washer
  and nut. The outside rib and sole do not need support.
- Do not scale to adjust fit: clear any burrs in the M8 passage instead.

## Installation

1. Print two short feet and two long feet. Remove the nut-pocket supports.
2. Place the short feet at the two holes nearer the non-motor end; their toes
   point toward that end. The tall round bosses contact the underside of the
   metal plate.
3. Place the long feet at the two holes nearer the motor; toes point toward it.
4. For each hole, insert a bolt and washer from above. Fit a washer and nut in
   the underside recess, then tighten gently. Align all four toes parallel to
   the plate's long edges before tightening.
5. Set the plate on a flat desk. Confirm all four soles sit flat, no fastener
   touches the desk or feet, and the assembly does not rock before a hand-spin
   check. Thin rubber pads on all four soles can help grip if needed.

The two short feet point toward the non-motor edge; the two long feet point
toward the motor edge. All four attachment bosses face inward along the shaft axis.

## Verification and limits

Both STL meshes were generated by OpenSCAD 2021.01 and checked for closed,
consistently oriented manifold surfaces, one connected component, positive
volume, non-degenerate triangles and expected dimensions. A separate OpenSCAD
intersection check found no collision with the eight fastener envelopes above.

This is a new, unprinted prototype. Physical fit, print strength, friction and
tipping resistance have not been tested. Use for unloaded desk assembly and
rotation checks; these feet are not an anchor for tensioned cable/load tests.

mkdir -p hardware/generated
