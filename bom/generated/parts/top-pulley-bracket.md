## Requirements

- 150 × 40 × 150 mm equal-leg solid angle, 5 mm galvanized steel. One per post: vertical leg through-bolted to timber; horizontal leg projects inward. Drill/enlarge two Ø13 mm mounting holes and one centred Ø9 mm shackle hole ~25 mm from the outer end for ~125 mm pulley offset.

## Notes

Drill-only V1 solution; no welding. Deburr and recoat new holes with zinc-rich spray. Controlled proof-load required before overhead use.
